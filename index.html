FAQs

Frequently Asked Dental Questions

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a checkup every 6 months, or more frequently if you have pain, sensitivity, or ongoing treatment needs.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes. Professional whitening under a dentist’s care is safe and effective, and we always evaluate your teeth first.

Can I get a root canal without pain?

Modern root canal treatment is designed to be comfortable, using local anesthesia and careful techniques to minimize discomfort.

Do you treat children?

Yes, we offer pediatric dentistry with gentle care tailored for kids and families.

How do I book an appointment?

You can call us, message via WhatsApp, or use the appointment form on this page for a quick response.
